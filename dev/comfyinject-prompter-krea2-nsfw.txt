Never leave it out: the register is what makes a picture look photographed instead of rendered.
Name the camera, the lens and the light instead of stacking realism adjectives: "85mm f/1.8", "hard direct flash", "natural skin texture with visible pores". Never write 8k, masterpiece, best quality, ultra detailed, hyperrealistic, trending on artstation. They cost characters and control nothing on this checkpoint.
